# Media cover illustrations

These are editorial illustrations, not documentary photos or data visualizations.
They were created with the built-in ImageGen tool and exported as WebP for the site.
Cards that use them are labelled in both languages. Photos supplied by the site
owner or obtained from the original coverage are stored separately under
`assets/media/mentions/`.

## Generation prompts

- `assets/media/illustrations/demographic-research.webp`: Generate one landscape 3:2 editorial illustration for an academic demographer's website, used as a thumbnail for demographic research and public policy. Sophisticated tangible paper-cut and printmaking still life: overlapping muted teal and cream sheets, simple anonymous population-pyramid bars and a small abstract outline map, ink charcoal with a single ochre accent, morning light, clean editorial crop, no text, no labels, no numbers, no flags, no seals or institutional branding. Looks clearly illustrative, not a documentary photograph or actual data visualization. The main subject is centered and clear at thumbnail size.
- `assets/media/illustrations/population-ageing.webp`: One landscape 3:2 editorial illustration for a media story about population ageing and intergenerational life, not a photograph. A dignified older woman and an older man walking together in a spacious city park, a younger adult with a child visible farther away. Modern hand-printed magazine illustration: nuanced sage and graphite ink, dusty warm coral and ivory paper, subtle texture, unforced natural poses and elegant architectural context. Clear central grouping that crops gracefully to a thumbnail. No type, no numbers, no charts, no badges, no logos; generic setting, no real people.
- `assets/media/illustrations/academic-exchange.webp`: A single landscape 3:2 editorial illustration for an academic site, for stories about university visits and scholarly exchange. A stylized university courtyard, warm tuff-like masonry and archways, an open book in the foreground and several tiny anonymous silhouettes walking towards the courtyard. Subtle regional Armenian architectural inspiration without depicting or claiming to show any real university, generic non-documentary setting. Sophisticated screen-print and paper texture, ink blue, terracotta and ivory, clean distinct composition with central focus, no readable text, logos, flags, emblems or signage.
- `assets/media/illustrations/family-health.webp`: Landscape 3:2 clearly illustrative editorial image for a demography archive on reproductive health, well-being, and the next generation. A visual metaphor, not a literal event: two adult hands sheltering a small leaf and a line of softly layered human silhouettes, a discreet abstract heart shape in negative space, delicate colored pencil and hand-printed paper collage. Muted forest green, fresh ivory and brick-red accents with airy composition. Strong readable centered shapes for a small media card, no text, numbers, branding or real people.
